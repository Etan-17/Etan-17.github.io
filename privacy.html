Privacy Policy — Clipping Bot

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What this app is

Clipping Bot is a personal, single-user tool. It uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people sign in.

Data accessed

  • YouTube: with the owner's permission (OAuth), the app uses the youtube.upload scope to upload videos and youtube.readonly to read the owner's own channel and video statistics.
  • Instagram: with the owner's permission, the app publishes Reels to the owner's own account and reads basic profile information.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer or server, in files readable only by the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Clipping Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking access in your Google or Instagram settings stops all access immediately. The owner can delete the stored tokens at any time by deleting the app's secrets folder.

Contact

Questions: use the support email listed on this app's Google consent screen.
